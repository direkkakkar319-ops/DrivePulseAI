[RAW 1.54GB] Nissan Leaf 2018 1 Year REAL-WORLD collected data:Dataset_NissanLeaf-2021_2022.csv

Extensive data-set of 4.5M samples collected by sampling the Electric Control Units (ECUs) in 250ms with a IoT OBD2 device in Nissan Leaf Model 2018
It explores the recorded 7,100 Km data, where 3.35\% degradation on the entire battery pack was monitored for 384 days
From: 2020-11 to 2021-12
